Each runner measures one fixed commit every day. An event is an environment change between two daily measurements (for example a kernel update or a harness deploy). Each event is tested against the daily scores and against paired sweep results. "No evidence of change" and "not enough data" are different results.

Consistency

States

  • confirmed: enough data, the interval excludes zero, q below 0.05 and the effect reaches the runner floor.
  • suspected: a signal that is not confirmed yet.
  • not enough data: too few days or pairs, or an interval still too wide to tell.
  • suspected (fleet-wide): several runners moved the same way on the same day; not counted as open.
  • explained: an operator wrote an explanation; the numbers stay visible.
  • no evidence of change: the interval is inside the runner floor. "Small shift below the floor" means a real but small shift.

Effect is the change of the median daily score after the event against the median before it. The bar shows the 95% interval (line), the effect (dot), zero (tick) and the runner floor (shaded); its scale is shown in the shown-count line. The data cannot tell which of several fields that changed together caused an effect.
