A PR is one commit on the swept branch; PR sets B to that commit and A to its parent, and so does leaving A empty. When a commit was not measured for a series, the cell uses the nearest measured point on the correct side and marks the drift. Throughput cells use a Welch test on the per-point cv/reps (p < 0.05 and |Δ| ≥ 1%). Perf-counter points carry no per-point spread, so their floor is the robust spread of adjacent-commit ratios over the trailing 30 points of the same counter scope. Two tiers: a cell clears at 2× that floor (colour), and is strong (‼) at 3× (p < 0.001 for throughput). Calibration over 24.8k commit pairs: the 2–3× band reproduces on another platform about one time in three, 3× and up about half or better. Grey means inside the floor.
